About Our Project

What our dictionary does and why we built it.

WordWise, our Dictionary Web Application, helps users search for English words and retrieve their meanings quickly. It connects to the free Dictionary API, so every result shown on the page comes from real data returned by the API.

Search Any Word

Type an English word and press Enter or click Search.

Clear Definitions

See the meanings of the word, organized neatly by definition.

Pronunciation

Phonetic spelling and audio are shown when the API provides them.

Parts of Speech

Noun, verb, adjective, and more, so you know how a word is used.

Examples & Synonyms

Example sentences and similar words appear when available.

Friendly Errors

Helpful messages appear if a word is not found or the request fails.
